# Yandex Direct RSYA Photo Prompts For Teen Intensive

## Technical Sizes

Use two primary image families for Text & Image / RSYA ads:

- Standard square: `1000x1000`, `1200x1200`, or `1500x1500`.
- Widescreen: `1778x1000` or `1920x1080`.

For future static image banners, prepare optional high-density exports:

- `600x500` for `300x250`;
- `900x750` for `300x250`;
- `600x1000` for `300x500`;
- `900x1500` for `300x500`;
- `600x1200` for `300x600`;
- `900x1800` for `300x600`;
- `672x560` for `336x280`;
- `1008x840` for `336x280`;
- `480x800` or `720x1200` for `240x400`;
- `960x640` or `1440x960` for `480x320`.

Format:

- JPG or PNG.
- Keep source files under 10 MB for Text & Image ads.
- For image ad banners, compress final files to 512 KB if using fixed image creative format.
- Avoid text on image. If text is required, keep it below 20% of image area.
- Do not use medical symbols, clinic settings, crying teenagers, fear-based scenes, logos, readable text, or fake UI buttons.

## Generation Style

Use a realistic editorial-photo style:

- bright modern studio or classroom;
- Moscow city daylight mood, but no recognizable private faces unless model releases exist;
- calm educational workshop, not therapy/clinic;
- teenagers 14-17 and adult facilitator, but no medical framing;
- natural expressions, no exaggerated joy, no sadness, no fear;
- no visible brand logos, no readable text.

## Core Negative Prompt

`no doctors, no clinic, no hospital, no medical symbols, no diagnosis, no therapy couch, no crying, no fear, no pressure, no conflict, no dark dramatic lighting, no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no fake buttons, no social media interface, no distorted hands, no extra fingers`

## Shooting Brief If Real Photos Are Made

Required frames:

- wide room shot with chairs in a circle;
- teenagers at table with blank cards;
- facilitator explaining a task;
- teen near flipchart;
- pair dialogue;
- laptop/AI-practice scene;
- flat lay: notebook, cards, markers;
- exterior or interior hint of city/studio;
- neutral portrait of facilitator;
- no close-ups of minors without signed parent consent.

Consent:

- Do not use recognizable photos of teenagers without written consent from parents/legal representatives.
- If consent is not available, use non-identifiable angles: backs, hands, silhouettes, cropped hands with materials, empty room and workshop objects.
