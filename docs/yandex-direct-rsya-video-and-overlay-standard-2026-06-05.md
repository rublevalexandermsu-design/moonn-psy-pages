# Yandex Direct RSYA Creative And Video Standard - Teen Intensive

Date: 2026-06-05

Scope: `https://xn--l1acaw.xn--p1ai/podrostkovyy-lager-psihologiya`

## Verified Platform Rules

- Yandex Direct supports short video in ads: 5-60 seconds.
- Recommended video aspect ratios: 16:9, 1:1, 9:16.
- Yandex recommends several video creatives in different ratios in one ad group to increase coverage.
- The key idea should be visible in the first 6-10 seconds because users may skip or scroll.
- Video should work without sound: use visual structure, captions and readable on-screen text.
- Image/banner text is allowed, but logos, watermarks and text on the image should not dominate the creative. Yandex recommends keeping such elements under 20% of the image area.
- Any price, date, place, discount or promise in the ad must match the landing page.

Sources:

- `https://yandex.com/support/direct/ru/efficiency/video`
- `https://yandex.ru/support/direct/ru/moderation/ad-rules`
- `https://yandex.com/support/direct/ru/moderation/adv-rules`

## Creative Strategy

Use three layers, not one generic photo pack:

1. Human trust: Tatiana Munn with a small teenage group in a bright room.
2. Offer clarity: short overlay text with age, dates, city and format.
3. Moderation safety: no medical framing, no fear pressure, no promises of psychological effect.

Do not make New League the main brand. It can appear as a location cue in the background, but the ad object is the teen intensive by Tatiana Munn.

## First Test Matrix

### Static Images

Use current prepared images and add 2-3 overlay variants:

- `14-17 лет`
- `Москва, 6-10 июля`
- `Речь, общение, эмоции, ИИ`
- `5 дней очно`

Avoid price in first overlay test. Price can be used later only after payment path and landing price are rechecked immediately before moderation.

Sizes:

- `1200x1200` square for broad RSYA coverage.
- `1200x675` horizontal 16:9.
- Keep text away from the top-right/top-left corners and edges.
- Keep overlay text under 20% of the image area.

### Video

Minimum set:

- `1920x1080`, 16:9, 10-15 seconds.
- `1080x1080`, 1:1, 10-15 seconds.
- `1080x1920`, 9:16, 10-15 seconds.

Structure:

1. 0-2 sec: bright room, group, Tatiana Munn, overlay `Интенсив для подростков 14-17`.
2. 2-6 sec: flipchart/cards/laptop, overlay `Речь • общение • эмоции • ИИ`.
3. 6-10 sec: New League / Moscow venue cue, overlay `Москва, Сущёвский Вал, 56`.
4. 10-15 sec: group activity, overlay `6-10 июля 2026` and button-style `Посмотреть программу`.

## Do Not Use

- Sensitive labels on cards as the main visual, especially `тревога`.
- Selfie-style photos with minors as the main ad creative.
- Visible name tags, personal data or recognizable school-like labels.
- Medical words: `лечим`, `терапия`, `диагноз`, `избавим`, `гарантия результата`.
- Fear pressure: `ваш подросток не справится`, `срочно`, `опасно`, `проблемный подросток`.

## Launch Gate

Before upload or moderation:

- Landing page contains the same dates, price, age, address and format.
- Metrika goals exist and at least click goals are tested.
- Payment path opens correctly.
- No creative includes unverified seat count or false urgency.
- Any use of recognizable minors has owner-side consent.
